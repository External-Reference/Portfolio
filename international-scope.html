International scope

External Reference has a strong international presence. Over the years, the studio has developed projects across Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and the Americas, working in cities such as Barcelona, Rome, Manchester, London, Paris, Dubai, Doha, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Jeddah, Seoul, Bangkok, Shenzhen, Guadalajara, Mexico City, Punta Ballena, and many more.

This global scope is closely tied to the relationships the studio has built with its clients. Many of External Reference’s projects emerge from long-term collaborations with international brands, cultural institutions, and private developers who trust the studio to shape unique spatial experiences. These partnerships allow the studio to work across different markets and cultures while maintaining a consistent design vision.

Through this network of clients and collaborators, External Reference continues to expand its presence worldwide, approaching each project as an opportunity to create meaningful architecture that responds to its context while expressing a clear and distinctive identity.
