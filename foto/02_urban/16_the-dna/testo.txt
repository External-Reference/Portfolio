Title: The DNA
Place: Dubai, UAE, 2025
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Dubai, UAE
Status: Concept design proposal

The DNA towers symbolize continuity and legacy in Dubai. Two forms intertwined like strands of DNA, representing the transmission of values, vision, and identity from one generation to the next. Rooted in the strength of the past and rising toward the future, the towers embody the evolution of heritage into innovation.

Each façade reads as a living archive, materials and light tracing the passage from origin to horizon. An architectural expression of family, unity, and progress, standing as an enduring landmark of intergenerational identity.
