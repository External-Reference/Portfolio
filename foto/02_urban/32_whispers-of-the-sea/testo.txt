Title: Whispers of the Sea
Place: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, 2022
Category: Public Space
Tags:
Location: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
Typology: Urban art installation in public space
Area: 1,330 m²
Status: Concept design proposal
Collaboration: Time Entertainment

Like a wave of art flooding the streets, Whispers of the Sea explores the city’s past through traditional folktales, as if told by the grandmothers of Jeddah in whispers coming from the sea. The trail is designed for adults, families, and children, with a total length of approximately 1,330 m.

It includes 11 pitstops across Al Balad, with 6 main stops hosting projections and interactive experiences. Physical installations offer glimpses during the day, and a multimedia approach culminates in the experience at night, with dedicated audio echoing the voices that recount local tales.
