Title: Confidential One
Place: Undisclosed, 2022
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Undisclosed
Area: 2,236 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

Set within a steep terrain overlooking the sea, the house is conceived as a dialogue between architecture and landscape. The concept explores the idea of a futuristic sculptural residence, resembling a spacecraft anchored to the mountain at only two points and extending outward towards the horizon.

Suspended above the slope, the volume creates a sense of movement and lightness, transforming the house into an iconic architectural object that merges technology, nature, and an exceptional coastal setting.
