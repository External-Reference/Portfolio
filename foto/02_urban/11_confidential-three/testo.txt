Terraform emerges as a monumental reconfiguration of the natural landscape, a bridge between two sandstone hills carved by time. Inspired by erosion and geological forces, it introduces a digital reconstruction of the terrain, where cascading pixelated terraces echo the patterns of nature’s wear and flow.

The intervention transforms the valley into an inhabitable terrain, a ridge that rises and falls with the landscape’s contours. As visitors ascend through this layered topography, they encounter spaces for contemplation, where the desert meets the sea and the ancient dialogue between earth and time finds its contemporary voice.
