Title: Jeddah Fashion Island
Place: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, 2019
Category: Mixed Use
Tags:
Location: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
Typology: Fashion & commercial complex masterplan
Area: 50,000 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

Set along Jeddah’s waterfront, Fashion Island embodies the harmony of permanence and motion, with a silhouette inspired by dunes and waves, reflecting the region’s heritage and fashion’s dynamic nature.

The island features expansive spaces for fashion shows, with sweeping arcs and adaptable canopies, hosting everything from grand runways to intimate couture showcases. Integrated retail zones surround them, while an F&B promenade offers dining with waterfront views and an entertainment zone provides activities for all ages.
