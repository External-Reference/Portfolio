Mirage is a cubic structure on the coastline, set against the desert landscape and a coral reef offshore. Its design balances solidity and transparency, with tall, narrow windows that evoke desert light. Reflective surfaces create a levitating illusion, aligning the cube’s midpoint with the horizon to connect earth and sky.

The fractured façade reads as resilience and adaptation, echoing the enduring character of desert cultures. Conceived as a contemplative living space, Mirage bridges cultural heritage and contemporary design.
