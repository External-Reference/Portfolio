Title: Confidential Two
Place: Undisclosed, 2022
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Undisclosed
Area: 2,130 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

The house pays homage to its dramatic natural setting, conceived as a sequence of crystalline formations that emerge directly from the rock, as if shaped by the geological landscape itself. These sculptural volumes rise from the cliff, creating a seamless fusion between architecture and nature.

Carefully carved openings frame the sea while strengthening the connection with the surroundings. The house becomes more enclosed along its sides, blending into the rock, while opening completely towards the horizon to create a continuous dialogue between interior, landscape, and sea.
