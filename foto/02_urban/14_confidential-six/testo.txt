The Enclave rises atop a flat hill on a small peninsula, where cliffs meet the sea to form a secluded refuge. Like sandstone formations shaped over millennia, the mansion is built as a series of horizontal slabs, layers that echo the geological rhythms of the land.

This stratification becomes the guiding concept, drawing on hidden layers of time to craft a structure in dialogue with its surroundings. Rooted in the earth yet opening toward the horizon, The Enclave integrates with the surrounding topography, its geometry honouring the passage of both time and place.
