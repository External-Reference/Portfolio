The residential building on Avenida del Libertador in Buenos Aires blends natural and architectural elements, reinterpreting Argentine brutalism in a contemporary and sustainable way. The façade, with integrated planters along balconies and windows, creates a green envelope that enhances both aesthetics and performance.

The brutalist foundation, with sharp geometric forms and raw materials, is softened by vegetation. The plants provide shade, privacy, and natural cooling, reducing energy use and reliance on air conditioning, while improving air quality and residents’ wellbeing.
