Title: Waves Festival
Place: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, 2022
Category: Mixed Use
Tags:
Location: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
Typology: Urban trail
Area: 100,000 m²
Status: Concept design
Collaboration: Time Entertainment

The Waves festival celebrates Jeddah’s coastal spirit across three zones: Aquatica, Legends of the Sea, and Urban Beach. Aquatica offers a 1 km floating trail with 10 multimedia worlds and large-scale installations exploring the connection between humanity, land, and sea. Legends of the Sea presents a water-fountain show inspired by Arabian folktales.

Urban Beach spans a vast coastal area with the Red Sea as its backdrop, hosting seven activity zones for up to 20,000 visitors. Designed for seamless crowd flow, it transitions from daytime activities into nighttime light displays, uniting people through the vitality of Jeddah’s shores.
