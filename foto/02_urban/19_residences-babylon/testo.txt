Title: Residences Babylon
Place: El Salvador, 2025
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: El Salvador
Status: Concept design proposal

Babylon represents a new way of living: a vertical landscape where architecture, technology, and nature coexist. Layered like suspended gardens, the towers merge ingenuity and ecological design to create elevated worlds of greenery and light.

Terraces cascade upward in tiers, each one a threshold between structure and open sky, blurring the line between building and landscape, and allowing nature itself to ascend as a living, breathing ecosystem.
