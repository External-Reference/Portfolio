Title: Cementerio Vespella de Gaià
Place: Vespella de Gaià, Spain, 2007
Category: Mixed Use
Tags:
Location: Vespella de Gaià, Spain
Area: 50,000 m²
Status: Concept design proposal, awarded

Every culture develops its own responses to death, defining rituals of reverence and methods of preservation. Ancestral Etruscan practices echo in the approach: tombs were seen as the dwellings of the dead, built as cylindrical structures integrated with the landscape.

Natural light is the key element: light from above symbolizes the connection between life and the afterlife and functions as a natural clock, as in Rome’s Pantheon. The proposal combines crater-type tombs, intimate lower-level spaces where only the sky is visible, with built micropantheons resembling small tholoi or funerary amphoras for family tombs, and a cemetery church that completes a contemplative landscape.
