Rising from its ancient slumber deep within the mountain, the Dormant Meteorite emerges, unveiling subtle glimmers of light from its core. As it ascends, it offers inhabitants unobstructed views over the coastline and the open sea.

The formation lifts its occupants higher with every moment, immersing them in the landscape that unfolds below: a panoramic journey through sea, mountains, and sky, designed for reflection from an elevated, otherworldly vantage.
