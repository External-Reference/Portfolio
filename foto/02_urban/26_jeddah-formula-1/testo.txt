Title: Jeddah Formula 1
Place: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, 2023
Category: Mixed Use
Tags:
Location: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
Typology: Formula 1 track masterplan
Area: 200,000 m²
Status: Concept design
Collaboration: Time Entertainment

The project for the F1 Grand Prix in Jeddah features four villages, Main Village, Central Village, Sports Village, and Art Promenade, embodying the SAGP motto “Overtake the Future.” Each village has a distinct design and personality within a single immersive experience.

Visitors move between lounges, food courts, shopping areas, and thematic activations. Exhibition zones showcase technology, while giant inflatable sculptures scattered across the site act as visual landmarks, turning the event into a festival landscape that celebrates innovation, art, and entertainment.
