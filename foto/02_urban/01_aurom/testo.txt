Title: Aurom Residences, D11
Place: Dubai, UAE, 2025
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Dubai, UAE
Typology: Residential urban design
Area: Masterplan 42,000 m², villas 1,150 m²

District 11 at Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum City is a carefully planned master community that embraces the rhythm of nature at the heart of Dubai. Designed for families and individuals seeking balance and wellbeing, it redefines premium living through a closer connection with the landscape. A new expression of luxury shaped by design and nature.

Within this context, Aurom is designed as a sanctuary where water, greenery, and luxury architecture coexist in perfect harmony. Its expansive villas create a landscape of privacy, light, and space. Each residence opens onto private gardens and outdoor living areas, encouraging a daily connection with nature.

The central feature of the Aurom Villas is the water landscape, a space where water shapes the atmosphere and experience, enhancing the aesthetic and sensory quality of the community by bringing movement, reflection, and sound.
