Title: Escalera y Mirador Vela
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2022
Category: Public Space
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Typology: Urban design
Area: 800 m²
Status: Built
Client: Nova Bocana
Engineering: PIGRA

Located along Barcelona’s waterfront near the W Hotel, the project introduces a sculptural staircase and elevated walkway that connect two levels of the promenade. Inspired by Mediterranean salt crystal formations, the intervention creates a new façade and public balcony that extends the promenade toward the sea while framing views of the coastline and city.

The intervention uses the existing seawall as both support and context, turning a former dead end into an urban connection. Its prefabricated reinforced concrete structure includes a 48-step stairway and an elevated walkway, clad with triangular bio-receptive panels developed through parametric design. The panels embed native plants such as Lobularia maritima, Lampranthus spectabilis, and Drosanthemum floribundum. A fiber optic lighting system activates the façade at sunset.
