Title: Al Layali Villa
Place: Dubai, UAE, 2022
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Dubai, UAE
Area: 751.31 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

The house is conceived as a composition of four cubic volumes connected by a triple-height space that becomes the central core of the project. Within this space, an organic sculptural staircase acts as the focal element, connecting the different levels of the house.

Inspired by Arab architecture and the spatial qualities of mosques, the design reinterprets the traditional arch as a contemporary architectural element. The façade arcades transform the classical arch into a three-dimensional sculpted feature, creating a dialogue between heritage and modern expression.
