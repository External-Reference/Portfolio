Title: Malinska
Place: Malinska, Croatia, 2020
Category: Hotel & Resort
Tags:
Location: Malinska, Croatia
Typology: Resort masterplan
Area: Landscape 250,000 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

Located in Malinska, the resort is conceived as a vibrant community that brings together leisure, wellness, culture, and nature. The complex includes gardens, sports facilities, retail, food and beverage spaces, and a medical center. Designed as a year-round destination, it combines architecture, landscape, and advanced health services to strengthen Malinska’s position within international tourism.

The resort serves as an experiential hub, featuring workshops and an amphitheater for performances, music, and events. As Malinska’s first five-star resort, it offers luxury apartments with direct access to shared amenities. By combining hospitality, culture, and infrastructure, it aims to become a new reference point for Croatian tourism while supporting the growth of nearby communities.
