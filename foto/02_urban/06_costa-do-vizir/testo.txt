Title: Costa do Vizir Wellness Complex
Place: Setúbal, Portugal, 2024
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Setúbal, Portugal
Area: 1,200 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

Stone walls hold the silence of a quiet luxury villa immersed in the Mediterranean nature. Local elements and materials trace the line between architecture and landscape, filtering light through deep overhangs and framing views toward the serene outdoor spaces.

Pools, gardens, and interior volumes are arranged to slow the rhythm of the day, shaping a wellness retreat where material honesty and spatial restraint become the true luxury.
