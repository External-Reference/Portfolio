Title: Magok Lake Park
Place: Seoul, South Korea, 2008
Category: Mixed Use
Tags:
Location: Seoul, South Korea
Area: 1,170,000 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

Magok Waterfront blends nature and artificiality to create a sustainable space of coexistence. Part of the Han River Renaissance Project, it redefines Magok’s identity through waterscapes, transport, and eco-formations, integrating water terracing, expansive wetlands, and recreational amenities.

A river barrage transforms the bay into freshwater, with sluice gates, fish passes, and flood defenses. Wetlands along the lake edges serve as ecological buffers and transition zones, while oxygenation, salinity control, and debris removal manage water quality, harmonizing ecology, infrastructure, and recreation.
