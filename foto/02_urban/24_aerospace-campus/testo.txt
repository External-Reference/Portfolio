The Aerospace Campus is a redevelopment project for the Montaudran Aerodrome, designed to increase efficiency in the production, education, and research of the European aerospace industry. Located near the Rangueil Scientific Campus and Paul Sabatier University, the development includes office space, research facilities, leisure facilities, and retail.

Home to the Latécoère factory and the Aéropostale services, the Montaudran Aerodrome is closely associated with Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, one of the great pilots of the twentieth century.
