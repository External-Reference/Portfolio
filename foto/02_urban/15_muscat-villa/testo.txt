Title: Muscat Villa
Place: Muscat, Oman, 2022
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Muscat, Oman
Typology: Private villa
Area: 2,000 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

Located on the Gulf of Oman, the Muscat Villa combines privacy, openness, and futuristic design. Conceived as a protective shell against the harsh climate, the residence frames expansive coastal views while blending with the arid landscape. The villa becomes a sculptural sanctuary immersed within the Omani coastline.
