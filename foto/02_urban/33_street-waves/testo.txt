Title: Street Waves
Place: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, 2022
Category: Public Space
Tags:
Location: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
Typology: Urban trail
Area: 5,300 m²
Status: Concept design proposal
Collaboration: Time Entertainment

Street Waves brings urban art installations to Jeddah’s pedestrian areas, embodying the city’s coastal spirit. The pieces simulate wave motion through 3D printing techniques, releasing gentle sprays of water to refresh passersby.

At night, the installations become multimedia displays, with video projections on mist curtains and augmented reality components. They also host the city-wide Augmented Reality Art exhibit, connecting Jeddah’s art community with residents and visitors.
