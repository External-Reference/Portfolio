Title: Al Khawaneej Villa
Place: Dubai, UAE, 2022
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Dubai, UAE
Area: 751.31 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

A luxurious villa where arches repeat like a colonnade of light, guiding the way through shaded thresholds into the landscape beyond. Each arch frames a different scene, a garden, a pool, a horizon, making each moment a form of discovery.

Inside, double-height volumes and refined finishes carry the same sense of scale and light, softening the line between built form and open land.
