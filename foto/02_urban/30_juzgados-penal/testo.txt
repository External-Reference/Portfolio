Title: Juzgados Penal
Place: Madrid, Spain, 2007
Category: Mixed Use
Tags:
Location: Madrid, Spain
Area: 28,000 m²
Status: Concept design proposal

The Criminal Court Building in Madrid’s Justice Campus is defined by its circular geometry, symbolizing fairness and equality. Its cylindrical form, with softened edges, conveys monumentality through simplicity. Precast concrete ensures neutrality and cohesion, while a double-skin façade adds flexibility without disrupting the serene exterior.

The base deforms slightly to create a welcoming, democratic entrance. Internally, concentric rings organize circulation, ensuring efficient movement between public and restricted areas while maintaining clear orientation.
