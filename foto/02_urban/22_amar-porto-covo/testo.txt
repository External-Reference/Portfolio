Title: Amar Porto Covo
Place: Porto Covo, Portugal, 2021
Category: Hotel & Resort
Tags:
Location: Porto Covo, Portugal
Typology: Resort masterplan
Area: 49,000 m²
Status: Processing building permit

The project envisions an Epicurean boho-chic hotel that reinterprets traditional hospitality. Seamlessly integrated into the landscape, the design maintains strong visual coherence while reducing environmental impact. Efficiency, energy performance, and ecological awareness guide the architecture.

Materials and construction methods follow sustainable principles and respect the local context. Where possible, the project uses local resources, including cork for finishes and structural elements, and rammed earth for the façade walls. These choices support local industries and help the building blend with its environment, while the landscape design responds to the relief, terrain, and natural character of the site.
