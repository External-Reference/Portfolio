Title: Residences Embrace
Place: Dubai, UAE, 2025
Category: Residential
Tags:
Location: Dubai, UAE
Status: Concept design proposal

Embrace expresses connection in distance. Two independent forms in perfect balance, separated yet deeply connected by a vertical space of light. This central void becomes a symbol of dialogue, growth, and mutual respect, reflecting Dubai’s character as a city where difference creates strength.

The two towers rise as mirrored volumes, their façades in quiet conversation across the gap that defines them. Rather than a divide, the void becomes an invitation, framing the open sky and drawing light into the space that finally unites them.
