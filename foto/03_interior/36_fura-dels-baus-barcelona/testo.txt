Category: Corporate

Location: Barcelona, Spain

La Fura dels Baus, a Spanish theatrical group, is known for its boundary-breaking performances that immerse the audience. Their office is designed as a biomorphic, luminous “stomach” that digests ideas, blurring the lines between acting and working. The space accommodates meetings, exhibitions, and performances, with an exterior area for storage and private meetings.

At its core is Meteorite, a glass and steel structure inspired by pallasite meteors. It houses 21 transparent capsules cultivating microalgae like Chlorella Vulgaris and Synechocystis 6803, which generate oxygen and absorb CO₂—producing 10 liters of oxygen per day, 8-10 times more than a conifer forest.

Created using computational design and traditional Murano glassblowing, Meteorite was presented at HOST Milano 2017, showcasing algae’s gastronomic potential with chef Graziano Prest.
