Title: Vicio Office
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2023
Category: Corporate
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Area: 1200 m2
Status: Built

This project transforms a former theater into a bold, modern office space reflecting VICIO’s youthful energy. Designed by Carmelo Zappulla and External Reference, the workspace fosters creativity, collaboration, and spontaneity. The entrance, inspired by VICIO’s restaurants, immerses visitors in the brand’s world. A cozy café welcomes guests, leading to the dynamic Playground—an interactive hub featuring sliding elements and a DJ station.

Surrounding it, elevated work areas turn employees into «actors» within this innovative setting. Former theater statues have been reimagined, while side balconies now function as connecting corridors. Meeting rooms, management offices, and administrative spaces complete the layout. A sleek white palette, accented by VICIO’s signature red, neon lights, and bold graphics, creates a playful yet professional atmosphere, embodying the brand’s spirit.
