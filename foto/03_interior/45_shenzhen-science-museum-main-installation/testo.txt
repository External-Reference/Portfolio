Fractal science reveals a universal geometric matrix that is self-evident throughout nature. We live in a universe of electromagnetic fractal waves: every branch of energy in the universe is fractal, and since everything is energy, everything is self-similar.

The branching organism is the symbol of the whole of nature, human beings and technology, and of the interconnection between living and artificial elements in the world. Located in the central part of the museum hall, it draws people in and involves visitors in an immersive, interactive experience.
