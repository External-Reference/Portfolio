Title: Vicio Almagro
Place: Madrid, Spain, 2023
Category: Restaurant
Tags:
Location: Madrid, Spain
Typology: Restaurant
Area: 180 m2
Status: Built

Vicio, one of Barcelona’s fastest-growing food delivery brands, expands into dine-in with its first physical location in Madrid. Inspired by contemporary urban culture and the brand’s bold identity, the design transforms the restaurant into an immersive environment. Digital billboards, dynamic lighting, sound, and art installations recreate the energy of the city, extending its atmosphere directly into the space.

The concept builds on Vicio’s audacious online presence and translates it into a spatial identity designed for future expansion. The interior is organized through a series of window displays that reveal key moments from the brand’s story, turning the restaurant into both a dining space and a brand narrative. On the lower level, the project shifts into a metro-inspired atmosphere, with metal infrastructure and neon signage that evoke an underground urban setting. This contrast between display, movement, and street culture creates a physical experience that reflects Vicio’s tone while giving the brand a recognizable architectural language.
