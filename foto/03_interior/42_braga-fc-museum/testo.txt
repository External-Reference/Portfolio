The Municipal Stadium of Braga connects nature and city, offering spectators views of the monumental quarry on one side and Braga's cityscape on the other. This fusion of football, nature and urban life sets the stage for the S.C. Braga Museum, which bridges the city's history with the club's legacy.

The museum invites fans to explore new ways to connect with their team while discovering Braga's evolution. Using the quarry as a central theme, the exhibition intertwines the team's achievements with the city's heritage, creating a unified narrative.

A standout feature is an immersive experience simulating match-day excitement. Through advanced audiovisual technology, fans can feel the stadium's energy, hearing chants, cheers and even vibrations, deepening their emotional bond with S.C. Braga. This holistic approach unites stadium, city and team in a single fan experience.
