The project for the exhibition of the new museum is based on the celebration of Real Madrid Football Club, its iconic players and its history.

The main concept was to transform a didactic journey into a fully interactive experience, where the digital is mixed with the physical through the latest technologies: digital fabrication, projection mapping, inflatable skins, dynamic mechanical displays, kinetic art installations, holograms, immersive rooms and interactive gaming rooms.
