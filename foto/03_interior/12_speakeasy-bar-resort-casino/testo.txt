The Speakeasy Bar at ARIA Resort & Casino in Las Vegas offers a hidden, immersive experience, accessible through the Presentedby store’s 3D-printed Nevada desert landscape and UFO, via an unmarked door. Inspired by art nouveau and parametric design, the bar’s intimate, organic forms and lush interiors in moody hues of brown and green create an atmosphere of awe. Central design elements include a dynamic bronze bar, organic tables, and green suede seating.

Proposal 1 features a ceiling with a graphic sky roof, bronze oval windows with graphic screens, and fabric-covered metal mesh walls. Seating includes velvet chairs and single-person booths, while organic shell patterns adorn the bronze tables and bar. Proposal 2 introduces carved wooden walls with ripple detailing and a centralized seating plan. The ceiling mirrors the graphic screens with metal mesh, blending fluidity and warmth. Proposal 3 highlights curved wooden strips extending to the ceiling, framing a central hallway. Behind the wood, layers of curtains, metal mesh, and lightboxes add depth and texture, uniting functionality with elegance.
