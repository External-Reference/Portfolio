Title: Va De Cuina
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2020
Category: Restaurant
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Typology: Deli Market
Area: 30 m2
Status: Built

Va de Cuina is a gastronomic concept store and retail boutique celebrating traditional, homemade, and local Catalan foods. In collaboration with chef Jordi Vila of Alkimia, the design draws inspiration from the brand’s honest materials and fresh ingredients. The space is divided into two areas: a gourmet takeaway section offering chef-prepared dishes and a boutique selling curated local ingredients.

The interior features walls and floors imprinted with traditional knit weaves, sealed with protective resin. A wooden counter, carved from a natural tree trunk, preserves its raw essence. The branding behind the counter reflects gourmet meals, Sarrià’s cultural heritage, and Vivanda restaurant’s influence. The storefront highlights the seasonality of the offerings, artistically displaying seasonal ingredients used in the products sold inside.
