Title: Bershka X Lollapalooza
Place: Paris, France, 2022
Category: Expo & Pavilions
Tags: 
Location: Paris, France
Typology: Pavilion
Area: 115 m2
Status: Built

External Reference was commissioned by fashion brand Bershka to design the Bershka Custom House at the Lollapalooza music festival in Paris. The stand upholds the unique identity of the brand: young-minded, bold, and real. The design incorporates a powerful graphic identity with Bershka’s customization technologies in a playful way to create and celebrate the experience of the customer and the brand. The eye-catching installation where Bershka’s newly developed physical-digital collection and their digital mascots Lil’ Kreets, which manifest as 3-meter-tall inflatables, are used as a canvas for the customization features of the collection.

The stand takes shape from the juxtaposition of the containers together with other primary architectural shapes, which are combined with a bold and colorful palette, drawing the attention of the festival attendees, inviting them to venture into the different zones that Bershka’s Custom House offers.

The customer journey starts on the ground floor at the customization lab where visitors can create unique designs on the screens. The experience is elevated and immersive when the visitor reaches the first floor which features a 3D Dressing Room Experience where they can try on and visualize themselves wearing digital garments created solely for this festival. The floor also features an art installation that fills the central patio with vibrant coloured fabric hanging from the metal structure for the stand. The rest of the space acts as a lounge where the visitors can hang out, and relax. This pop-up retail project draws attention to the need of innovation and customization in the ever-evolving and fast-changing retail sector.
