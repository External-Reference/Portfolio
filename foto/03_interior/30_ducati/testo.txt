Title: Ducati
Place: worldwide, 2022
Category: Retail
Tags:
Location: worldwide
Typology: General Retail Concept
Area: 500 m2
Status: Concept design proposal

Casa Ducati embodies modern heritage, race DNA, and Italian craftsmanship in a scalable layout. Its design is structured into five key areas: The «Tunnel», inspired by aerodynamic wind galleries, showcases Ducati’s history. The «Cuore Ducati» features Italian-designed furniture and a display table, presenting a special creation like an art exhibit. The «Hall of Fame» highlights top motorcycles on modular ramps, accompanied by matching apparel and accessories.

The «Racing Corner» displays iconic MotoGP bikes, followed by best-selling models showcased on a thin red carpet. The «Ducati Wall» presents exclusive brand products. Materials and colors red, black, gray, and the Italian tricolor reflect Ducati’s identity. Casa Ducati, designed by External Reference, celebrates the brand’s legacy while creating a global showcase for its stores.
