Tags:

Area: 1600 m2
Status: Concept Design Proposal

From the mountains to the coast, from tradition to the present, from Peru to the world. The pavilion is conceived as a holistic learning experience of Peruvian wisdom, formed by partial experiences lived in each room and structured as a journey across the three theme axes of Expo 2020 Dubai.

Mobility, through the Peruvian people: from the cradle of the Inca civilisation in the mountains to the coast, cradle of the oldest civilisations, and today from Peru to the world, through superfoods and gastronomy. Sustainability, as part of a Peruvian wisdom based on living in harmony with nature. Opportunity, through the promotion of innovation projects, and especially superfoods.
