Title: Yota Pavilion II
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2014
Category: Expo & Pavilions
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Typology: Trade-fair Pavilion
Area: 200 m2
Status: Built

Designed for Yota at the 2014 Mobile World Congress in Barcelona, this booth created a sensory experience through light, music and projections. A topographic ceiling, paired with rhythmic music, captured attention, while an eroded technological landscape below mirrored its design.

Inside, smooth ribbon-like forms served functional purposes, expanding to showcase devices or contracting for projection areas. The design integrated technical spaces and meeting rooms seamlessly within the flowing structure. The exterior featured a dark, perforated surface that revealed the glowing interior, creating a welcoming plaza where visitors could explore Yota's latest products.
