Inspired by desert oases, the store explores the physical and symbolic presence of water. You enter through a programmable water curtain before reaching a space shaped by flowing forms and cascading displays. 3D-printed walls, digital waterfalls, and interactive projections create an immersive environment where technology, sound, and movement transform the retail experience.

At the centre of the store, a bespoke water printer turns digital images into animated sculptures made from falling water, refreshing the atmosphere while creating a strong visual focus. Along the sides, product displays reinterpret cascades frozen in time, forming sculptural walls made with PLA and CO₂-capturing PureTech material. Pebble flooring strengthens the link with the oasis concept, while projected water reacts to each step. A mirrored corridor leads to a private VIP lounge, where screens and a reflective ceiling surround clients with liquid imagery for a more intimate experience.
