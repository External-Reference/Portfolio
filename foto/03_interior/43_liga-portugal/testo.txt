Title: Liga Portugal
Place: Porto, Portugal, 2024
Category: Museum & Exhibition
Tags: 
Location: Porto, Portugal
Typology: Exhibition
Area: 270 m2
Status: Built

The concept for the Liga Portugal museum in Porto offers visitors an immersive journey that mirrors a player's experience during a football match. As visitors enter the first space, they are greeted by a tunnel reminiscent of a stadium entrance, where the distant sound of cheering fans fills the air. This builds anticipation as they move towards the main exhibition area, designed to resemble the grandeur of a football stadium itself.

The next section takes visitors into a locker room setting, where they can explore the history and timeline of the league along with a display of trophies, continuing the feeling of being inside the stadium. Finally, visitors return through a tunnel-like space, symbolising the exit after the match, creating a full-circle experience that captures the essence of a player's journey before, during and after the game.
