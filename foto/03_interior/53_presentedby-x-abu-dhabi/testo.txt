Presented by debuts in Travel Retail with a new store at Abu Dhabi International Airport, in collaboration with Grupo Avolta. Designed by External Reference, the store offers an immersive shopping experience inspired by water as a symbol of calm in the bustling airport.

A digital waterfall marks the entrance, while 3D-printed panels mimic cascading water and shape the displays. At the center, a column resembling a fountain features a digital display simulating water particles, adding movement to the space.

Sustainability is central to the design: the 3D-printed panels use 70% recycled PET-G plastic, minimizing waste. The store also adopts a Design for Disassembly (DfD) approach, allowing materials to be reused. The concept combines a serene oasis with eco-conscious practices, promoting a circular economy.
