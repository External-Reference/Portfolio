A VIP lounge concept for travel retail, transformed into a travelling exhibition that allows potential clients to fully immerse themselves in the LX Lounge experience. The concept is articulated in four spaces, each developed in a full version and in a compact 30 m² format.

The Gallery is the vibrant heart of the VIP experience, where members are received amidst local art pieces. The Boutique is an innovative extension of the duty free store, presenting products in a unique and captivating way. Trends Trade is an immersive haven where travellers disconnect from the physical world and dive into a digital retail universe. Farm to Table is an open kitchen with live cooking stations, where guests can interact with the chefs.
