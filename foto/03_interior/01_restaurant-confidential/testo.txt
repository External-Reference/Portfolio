Perched above the city, the restaurant opens onto spanning views that unfold through fullheight glazing. A sculptural ceiling anchors the room, its sinuous form tracing overhead like a suspended wave, setting the tone for the space below. Timber and high end textiles bring warmth and texture to the volume, grounding its scale in materials chosen for their depth and finish. Together, form and finish create a dining experience where the city becomes part of the architecture itself, and every surface answers to the rhythm of the movement unfolding overhead.
