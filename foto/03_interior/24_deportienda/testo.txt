Deportienda at Abanca-Riazor Stadium transforms the official RC Deportivo de La Coruña store into an immersive retail space shaped by the energy of the club’s fans and the Atlantic landscape of A Coruña. A sculptural wave runs through the 250 m² interior, creating a continuous spatial experience that connects the “Onda Branca” with the movement and force of the ocean. The design treats the wave as both architecture and identity.

Made of 155 modules in recyclable PETG and produced through largeformat robotic 3D printing, the installation becomes the central element of the store, combining digital fabrication, sports culture, and local references. Modular furniture allows the space to adapt to different product categories, matchday crowds, seasonal activations, and changing retail configurations.

PureTech coatings help capture airborne pollutants and improve indoor air quality, while an artificial intelligence system developed by Noumena uses anonymous computer-vision data to analyze visitor flows, behaviors, and areas of interest, continuously optimizing the spatial and commercial experience. The result is a living retail environment that connects the identity of Deportivo, its supporters, and the city through material innovation, data, and Sustainability and technology are the Atlantic landscape. embedded into the architecture.
