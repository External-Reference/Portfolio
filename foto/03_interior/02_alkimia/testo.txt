Alkimia, a Michelin-starred restaurant in Barcelona, resides in a historic modernist building from 1864, unoccupied for four decades. The renovation honors the building’s heritage while aligning with Alkimia’s innovative gastronomy.

The design is a seamless interplay between past and future, featuring sea life-inspired skins, art installations, and custom furniture that create a dynamic, ever-evolving space. Respect for the existing architecture was key—no structural changes were made, and original wall paintings were preserved, adding historical depth to the modern design.

This blend of digital fabrication and traditional craftsmanship reflects Alkimia’s ethos, offering a sensory journey where history and innovation coexist. The result is a restaurant that is as rich and layered in design as its culinary creations.
