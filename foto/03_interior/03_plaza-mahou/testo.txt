The Mahou restaurant at Santiago Bernabéu celebrates Madrid’s culture uniting football, the city, and Mahou. Inspired by the social life of Madrid’s plazas, the space is organized around a central bar and a visible microbrewery. Sculptural ceilings, cobblestoneinspired floors, and panoramic views toward the stadium create a vibrant setting to gather and celebrate football culture.

The experience unfolds as a contemporary Madrid plaza inside the stadium. Visitors enter through an immersive tunnel inspired by the world of beer, before reaching a central bar that anchors the space as its social heart. Around it, the microbrewery becomes part of the scene, showing the brewing process as a live ritual.

Cobblestone-style flooring, embedded beer cans, steel tube ceilings, and 3D-printed panels translate the language of the city and the brand into material form. A panoramic window opens the restaurant to the pitch, while the terrace creates a relaxed place to meet, drink, and celebrate before and after the match.
