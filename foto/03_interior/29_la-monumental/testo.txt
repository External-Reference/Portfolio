Title: La Monumental
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2020
Category: Retail
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Typology: Cultual & Retail Center Masterplan
Area: 22 000 m2
Status: Concept design proposal

The Monumental building project transforms a former bull arena into a vibrant cultural center and shopping complex, blending historical preservation with innovative design. The original structure is preserved, maintaining its historical integrity, while a modern architectural element is suspended within the arena, creating a striking focal point. This new structure houses exhibition spaces celebrating the arena’s history and commercial areas offering a unique shopping experience.

At the arena’s base, a covered green area adds an inviting touch, enhancing the venue’s charm. The space between the new and old structures accommodates temporary commercial zones, providing opportunities for showcasing emerging brands and leveraging the arena’s distinctive layout. This project masterfully balances the old and new, creating a destination that honors its historical roots while embracing modern possibilities for cultural and commercial engagement.
