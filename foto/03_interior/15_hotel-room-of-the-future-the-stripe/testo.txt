Title: Hotel Room of The Future (The Stripe)
Place: Undisclosed, 2024
Category: Hotel
Tags:
Location: Undisclosed
Area: 24 m2
Status: Concept design proposal

A space built for versatility and comfort. It begins as a cozy living room, welcoming the guest with warmth and relaxation. However, the true innovation lies in the room’s ability to shift between various modes: from living room to bedroom, work space, or entertainment area. This is perfect for guests who want a home-like feel but also value customization.
