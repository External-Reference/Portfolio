Title: Expo 2020 Dubai India Pavilion Exhibition
Place: Dubai, UAE, 2021
Category: Expo & Pavilions
Tags: 
Location: Dubai, UAE
Typology: EXPO Exhibition
Status: Concept Design Proposal

The project's mission is to showcase India's future, innovation and research by turning a didactic exhibit into a sensory experience that goes to the heart of the visitor.

The exhibition is defined by a continuous, curved surface that identifies the different topics addressed in the space. Its organic shape produces a fluid connection from one space to the next, carrying the visitor towards an unexpected dimension. To achieve this, the floor is lifted 70 cm with a glass technical pavement floating over a stepped topography, a nod to India's topographic diversity and to its architectural step wells.

Visitors float over this topography, against a background screen that changes to create dramatic experiences coherent with the topic of each space.
