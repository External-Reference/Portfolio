Title: Moritz Rambla De Catalunya
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2021
Category: Restaurant
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Typology: Bar & Gift Store
Area: 420 m2
Status: Built

The Moritz factory, a historic brewery in Barcelona since 1856, underwent a transformation after its 2011 renovation by Jean Nouvel. Preserving the original shell, bar, and entrance shelves, the redesign embraced a brewer aesthetic aligned with hipster craftsmanship, reflecting Moritz’s craft beer identity. A new L-shaped bar, stretching six meters, was introduced, connecting the interior to the street through the window. This bar integrates six beer tanks three below and three above directly linked to the factory’s production tanks. Unpasteurized beer flows through long tubes, symbolizing the journey from production to consumption.

The design echoes Jean Nouvel’s material and aesthetic values while featuring limited-edition beers in one-liter bottles. The second part of the space, the M-store, showcases brand merchandise, gadgets, and books, with walls colonized for displays. Unique furniture crafted from tank sections completes the innovative, beer-focused experience.
