Title: European Union Pavilion
Place: Guadalajara, México, 2023
Category: Expo & Pavilions
Tags:
Location: Guadalajara, México
Typology: Exhibition Pavilion
Area: 1,130 m2
Status: Built

For the 2023 Guadalajara International Book Fair, the European Union Pavilion was designed under the theme “Building a Union of Cultures.” Inspired by the New European Bauhaus, it combines geometric forms, cultural spaces, and sustainable materials to create an experiential environment that strengthens ties between the EU, Mexico, and Latin America.

The pavilion brings together a bookstore, central agora, digital media library, and interactive installation within a cohesive spatial system. Colour and geometry help spatially define each function. A textile roof represents the EU member countries, adding rhythm, identity, and warmth to the interior.

Traditional sarape fabric creates a direct link with Mexican craft, while 3D-printed plants made from recycled plastic and PURE.TECH introduce a technological layer that absorbs CO₂. Designed with circularity in mind, most materials were planned for reuse or recycling.
