Title: Mestia Hotel
Place: Mestisa, Georgia, 2025
Category: Hotel
Tags:
Location: Mestisa, Georgia
Area: 10,000 m2
Status: Concept design proposal

The hotel is a vertical journey through a mountain’s natural layers. Each floor reflects a biome — from the rocky base (Lobby) to the snowy summit (Spa). Materials, light, and temperature shift with each level, offering a multisensory experience. The architecture mirrors altitude, blending nature and design.
