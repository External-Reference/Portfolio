Title: Spanish Pavilion, Yeosu Expo
Place: Yeosu, South Korea, 2012
Category: Expo & Pavilions
Tags:
Location: Yeosu, South Korea
Typology: Exhibition
Area: 1000 m2
Status: Built

At Expo Yeosu 2012, Spain presented “Spain Explores,” an exhibition celebrating its maritime exploration and scientific research. Through a sequence of immersive spaces, from historic sea routes to a deep ocean installation, the pavilion transformed nautical discoveries into a sensory experience while highlighting Spain’s commitment to ocean research and sustainability.

The exhibition unfolds in four stages, guiding visitors from cartographic routes to the depths of the ocean. The central narrative links Columbus, Malaspina, and the Malaspina 2010 expedition, showing how exploration evolved from navigation to scientific research. In the Deep Ocean space, water samples collected at 5,000 meters become the core material of the installation. Their forms recall laboratory tubes and amphorae, creating a glowing abyssal landscape activated by projected water movement. The journey ends with a Dymaxion map that turns scientific data into a visual field of discovery, linking knowledge, memory, and the future of the seas.
