Category: Retail
Tags:

Area: 1500 m2

The Clockwork, a four-story flagship store in Bangkok’s vibrant Thonglor district, is a bold expression of luxury, complexity, and precision. Designed as a colossal robotic mechanism, it showcases limited-edition sneakers and apparel through meticulously crafted spaces. The design mirrors the intricacy of watch gears, enhanced with 24-carat gold accents and a striking facade. A large transparent screen, resembling a watch case or vault door, projects brand content, blending innovation with visual impact.

The ground floor pulses with energy, featuring sneaker displays and a live DJ, while upper levels house clothing and exhibitions. The store’s mechanical aesthetic and hybrid elements create a dynamic and immersive experience.

At External Reference, we embrace challenges with passion and discipline, crafting innovative designs that redefine architectural boundaries, as exemplified by The Clockwork.
