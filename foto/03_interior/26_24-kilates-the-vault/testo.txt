Title: 24 Kilates - The Vault
Place: Bangkok, Thailand, 2015
Category: Retail
Tags:
Location: Bangkok, Thailand
Typology: Experiential Retail
Area: 250 m2
Status: Built

24 Kilates Bangkok transforms sneaker retail into an exclusive, emotionally engaging experience inspired by a luxury treasury. Vault-like interiors, safes, and concealed lockers create mystery and anticipation, encouraging discovery. The store enhances the allure of special-edition footwear from brands like Nike, Adidas, and Puma, strengthening the connection between customers, products, and brand identity.

The interior is organized through two contrasting atmospheres. A golden showroom, linked to the brand’s name, presents the products as precious objects within a secure and refined setting. From there, customers move into a darker area designed for trying on selected sneakers and hosting music events or DJ sessions. The use of gold and black reinforces the sense of luxury, secrecy, and access, turning each visit into a ritual of selection and reveal. The store builds desire through a controlled journey where products remain partly concealed until the customer actively discovers them.
