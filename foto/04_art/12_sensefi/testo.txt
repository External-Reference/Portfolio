Title: Sensefi
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2024
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Status: Built

Sensefi is the inaugural design of a collection developed for Barcelona’s urban furniture, introducing a flexible system that reimagines the relationship between public space and its users. Conceived as a modular prototype, each element can be combined with others to generate endless configurations, creating adaptable landscapes that encourage gathering, interaction, and rest.

Crafted from durable concrete, the design contrasts the material’s inherent solidity with soft, organic geometries that evoke the folds and fluidity of fabric. This balance between permanence and lightness transforms a functional urban object into a sculptural presence, offering both comfort and visual identity while adapting to the evolving needs of contemporary public spaces.
