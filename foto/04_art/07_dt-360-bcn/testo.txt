Title: DT-360-BCN
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2023
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain

The DT-360-BCN series translates invisible urban data into patterns.

DT-360-BCN-PS renders public safety data as waveforms — frequency marks recurrence, amplitude marks severity.

DT-360-BCN-AP maps electromagnetic, thermal, and atmospheric fields as vectors, generating the pattern’s lines from each point in space.

DT-360-BCN-DC visualizes the city’s daily commuter flows through particle and reaction-diffusion systems.
