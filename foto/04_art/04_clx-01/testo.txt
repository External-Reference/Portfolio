CLX by External Reference harmonizes urban living with nature through a chair collection inspired by the organic elegance of tree branches. Designed with sustainable materials and minimal waste, each piece combines craftsmanship, innovation, and fluid forms. Versatile for indoor and outdoor use, CLX creates a seamless connection between contemporary design and the natural world.

Each curve is shaped to suggest the quiet rhythm of natural growth, giving the chair a light yet expressive presence. Its branching logic creates a balance between strength, comfort, and visual softness, allowing the object to feel both technical and organic. CLX does not imitate nature directly; it translates its structure into a contemporary design language. Whether placed in a living room, terrace, hotel, or garden, the collection brings a sense of calm and continuity, turning everyday seating into a subtle encounter between body, material, and landscape.
