system combining 3D-printed elements with wooden connections, redefining product design and interior architecture. Prioritizing aesthetics, sustainability, and efficiency, 3D printing allows for customized adjustments in each piece. The main materials are concrete for an industrial look and PIPG, a sustainable polymer of 70% recycled plastic and 30% fiberglass, ensuring strength and durability.

Inspired by textile maquila processes, cyclical patterns in printing enable precise assembly with wooden elements. Each module balances functionality, strength, and geometry for maximum rigidity. This dynamic approach moves beyond static furniture, offering an expandable, adaptable design. By merging technology, creativity, and sustainability, External Reference challenges traditional permanence, demonstrating how design evolves in a changing world.
