Title: Lightscape
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2022
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain

“Light Scape” transforms Barcelona’s urban soundscape into an expressive light installation through parametric design and handcrafted neon technology. Based on field recordings converted into visual frequencies, the piece merges sound, light, and local craftsmanship. Presented at Casa Seat and Milan Design Week 2022, it creates a dynamic sensory experience blending innovation, experimentation, and atmosphere.

Each luminous line represents a specific frequency extracted from the city, turning invisible acoustic data into a spatial composition. The piece draws on the shared nature of sound and light as waves, creating a poetic link between urban memory and visual perception. Argon-filled neon tubes give the installation a living presence, with a glow that feels both precise and atmospheric. Through this process, Barcelona is not represented through images or landmarks, but through its rhythms, vibrations, and everyday sonic identity.
