Title: Mangrove Table
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2018
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain

We designed the Mangrove table for Sayrach´s house collection. The marine world is the main source of inspiration and Mangroves are trees that grow in environments where the water contains high concentrations of salts and water levels are regularly changing.

Growing where the substrate is unstable, Mangroves have developed an architecture of complex roots that rise from the main branch producing flying structures. This fractal structure has been studied and simulated computationally for the design of this 3D-printed table.

As the trees it is inspired by, this table is passively purifying the air of the room it is in. By incorporating PURE. TECH during the fabrication process our Manglar Table can absorb CO2 and neutralize VOCs and NOX present in the air.
