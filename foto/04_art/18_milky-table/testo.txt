Title: Milky Table
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2018
Tags:
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Typology: Furniture

Milky Table was designed for the meeting room of a renowned dairy company. Milk is an excellent medium for the growth of microorganisms: bacteria are employed to convert milk sugars into lactic acid, a process essential to the production of cheese and yogurt.

By monitoring bacterial communities in raw milk, we discovered intriguing white formations. These topographical morphologies were digitally simulated, modelled and translated into a Corian cast table.
