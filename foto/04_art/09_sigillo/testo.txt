Title: Sigillo
Place: Expo Osaka, Japan, 2025
Tags:
Location: Expo Osaka, Japan

Sigillo is a luminous sculpture that bridges the Mediterranean sun and the Japanese soul. Enclosed in a translucent crystalline block, a glowing red core holds light, memory, and warmth, echoing both the Japanese sun and the Mediterranean sunset. The work invites stillness and reflection, where subtle shifts of light reward a slow gaze. Through this quiet presence, Carmelo Zappulla creates a space for contemplation and cultural connection.

Its compact form carries a strong symbolic weight, balancing mineral clarity with an inner pulse of colour. The translucent body filters light like a captured atmosphere, while the red centre appears suspended between object and horizon. As viewers move around it, reflections and shadows shift, revealing new depths within the block. The sculpture becomes a small threshold between places and memories, where light acts as matter, symbol, and emotional trace.
