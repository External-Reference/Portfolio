This table is an exploration of pure forms and the balance between presence and weightlessness. Stripped down to its essence, it features a continuous, fluid top resting on two monolithic supports. These pillars ground the piece, creating a sense of strength and stability while allowing the top to appear almost suspended.

The design embraces simplicity, where geometry and proportion dictate its character without unnecessary embellishments. Transparency plays a key role, amplifying the contrast between the solidity of the base and the lightness of the top, creating a subtle tension between mass and air. The table is not just a functional object: it is an experience of clarity and precision, where form and space interact seamlessly. The result is a timeless piece, refined and intentional, defined by its sculptural yet understated presence.
