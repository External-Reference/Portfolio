Design Services

We are an award-winning studio that combines architecture, technology, and art to create spaces that tell stories, connect people and ideas, and create memorable and meaningful experiences. Our work moves from concept to materialisation, linking the potentials of imagination with those of precision, and developing projects that range from small objects to large urban environments.

Retail - Where architecture, technology, and storytelling shape product and brand discovery.

Hospitality - Restaurants, hotels, and leisure designs blending atmosphere, identity, and social interaction.

Corporate - Workplaces and headquarters that both reflect company culture and support new ways of working.

Residential - Merging strong architectural designs with a deep connection to landscape and lifestyle.

Urban Design - Rethinking public space and the relationship between architecture, mobility, nature and community.

Pavilions & World Expos - Pavilions and installations designed for international events where architecture drives storytelling and innovation.

Art & Collectable Design - Bespoke pieces that explore innovation in form, material, and meaning.

R&D - Experimental development of new technologies, materials, and design tools to expand the possibilities of architecture and experiential design.
