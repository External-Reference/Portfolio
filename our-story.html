Our Story

External Reference works from a clear premise: design is a process in constant evolution. Over two decades, the studio’s multidisciplinary team has grown into a practice with a distinct language that combines architecture, digital tools, and crafts, with experiential design and contemporary luxury at its core.

Each project is an opportunity to innovate and transfer the knowledge gained to future work. This approach allows us to integrate cutting-edge technologies into the creation of memorable and highly sophisticated spaces.

In projects such as the restaurant CAAA, in Lucerne, Switzerland, the Spectacular Suites at Hotel W and the waterfront Escaleras y Mirador both in Barcelona, we introduced AI and generative tools into the design and 3D modelling workflow, and Augmented Reality to connect the computational vision and built form with a high level of control and precision.

This technical expertise extends from the development of advanced material and engineering solutions to architectural innovation and adaptive urban planning, developed custom for highly demanding contexts. From the Pinko flagship store at Rome Fiumicino Airport, which led to the firm’s development and patenting of a custom fire-retardant coating that met strict airport regulations without compromising spatial quality or brand identity; to the combination of innovation, craftsmanship, and attention to detail that defines Plaza Mahou at the Santiago Bernabéu Stadium.

Escaleras y Mirador Vela, Nova Boccana, Barcelona, Spain (2022)

Or generative design and bespoke computation enhancing our extensive retail portfolio from mere shopping to a comprehensive experience. A long-term collaboration of more than seven years with brands such as PresentedBy, through international projects in Abu Dhabi and Doha, reflects our ability to transform commercial spaces into immersive, exclusive, and widely recognized destinations. ​

And finally, simulation and adaptive computation for the development of quality architecture, climate-conscious and resilient urban development from Porto Covo in Portugal, private villas in Dubai, and the Libertador building in El Salvador.

Across these works, luxury is understood as precision, refined materiality, quality architecture aesthetics, sustainable urban spaces carefully designed experiences. Our strength lies in a multidisciplinary approach and an adaptable transition between scales, from collectable object to refined building and adaptive urban landscape.

Today, External Reference is an award-winning and globally renowned firm that applies parametric computation, digital fabrication, new material development, and a culture of excellence rooted in luxury to deliver solutions that are precise, innovative, aesthetic and responsible.

From high-end residential and urban projects to corporate spaces, innovative architecture and international pavilions, we use storytelling as a tool to build clear and contemporary responses tailored to your project needs.

Onada Terrace Suites, Hotel W | Marriott Group, Barcelona, Spain (2025)
